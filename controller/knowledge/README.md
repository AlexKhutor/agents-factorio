# Knowledge

The controller's durable, reviewable record of what its agents delivered.

- `reports/inbox/<source-id>/` holds the reports desk agents submitted, copied
  byte for byte when the control cycle collects them, each with a
  `.meta.json` record of its source, revision and SHA-256.
- `catalog.json` is generated by `tools/rebuild_knowledge_catalog.ps1` from
  the files here and their hashes; report acceptance checks that an imported
  report is in it. Do not edit it by hand.
- `document-registry.json` gives stable IDs to the documents you want to
  keep under a fixed name. Every listed document must exist.

Reports are immutable once imported. Record your conclusions in a new
document rather than rewriting a report.
